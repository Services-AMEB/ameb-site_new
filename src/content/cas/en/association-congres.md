before:
  - "Each registration was copied from the form into a spreadsheet, then into the member list and the accounting system."
  - "Member rates were checked by hand, and invoices and receipts went out one at a time."
  - "The post-event survey went out late, and its responses sat in a separate file, with no link to participants."
system:
  - "Online registration form that automatically applies the member or non-member rate"
  - "A single record per participant, linked to the member list"
  - "Invoice and online payment at registration, with automatic reminders for unpaid balances"
  - "Automatic reminders to participants before the event"
  - "Post-event survey sent automatically, with responses attached to each record"
  - "Registration and revenue dashboard, continuously up to date"
changed: "No more re-entry between registration and invoice: each attendee is entered once, and the survey goes out on its own."
