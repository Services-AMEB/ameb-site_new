## The information we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you.

### Contact and diagnostic forms

- your name;
- your email address;
- the name of your organization;
- your message.

Our forms never ask for health information. Please do not include any in the message field.

### Calculator and self-assessment

The values you enter in the re-entry cost calculator (number of employees, hours, hourly cost) do not identify you. They appear in the page address so that you can share the result.

Your email address is collected only if you check the “Receive the details by email” box, which is never checked by default. It is then used solely to send you those details.

The self-assessment shows your result without asking for your name or email.

### Technical data

Like any website, this one receives technical connection data: IP address, browser type, page requested. Our host processes this data to deliver and protect the website.

## Why we use it

- To respond to your request and, if you wish, schedule a diagnostic.
- To send you the details of your calculation, if you asked for them.
- To protect the website and forms against spam and abuse.
- To measure website traffic in aggregate, in order to improve it.

We do not use your information for any other purpose without asking for your consent again. We do not add you to any newsletter without your separate agreement. We do not sell or rent your information.

No decision about you is made exclusively through automated processing [TO BE CONFIRMED].

## On what basis: your consent

We process your information with your consent. You give it when you submit a form, after reading the purpose stated below it. For any optional sending, a separate box, unchecked by default, leaves the choice to you. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see “Your rights”).

## Cookies and audience measurement

The website uses no non-essential cookies. That is why no cookie banner is displayed: as long as no tracker capable of identifying you is used, we consider that a banner is not required (position to be validated by a lawyer [TO BE CONFIRMED]). Should that change, we would ask for your consent before turning on such a tracker.

We measure audience with a tool that uses no cookies and no persistent identifier (tool: [TO BE CONFIRMED]). It produces aggregate statistics: pages viewed, referring website, device type, clicks on certain buttons (for example “Book my diagnostic”). These statistics are not used to follow you from one website to another or to build a profile of you.

## Our providers

### Cloudflare: hosting and spam protection

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. On pages that include a form, and only on those pages, we use Cloudflare Turnstile, a spam protection service that processes your IP address and signals from your browser to tell a person from a bot.

### Form relay

Submitted forms are relayed, through an encrypted and authenticated transmission, to an internal automation tool that forwards them to us (hosting: [TO BE CONFIRMED]).

### Appointment booking

The booking link leads to an external appointment booking service (tool: [TO BE CONFIRMED]). The information you enter there is processed by that service under its own policy, and by us under this policy.

Cloudflare may process data outside Québec and Canada. The law requires a privacy impact assessment before personal information is communicated outside Québec. This assessment has been carried out for our providers [TO BE CONFIRMED], and each communication is covered by a written agreement [TO BE CONFIRMED].

## Retention period

We keep messages received through the forms for [TO BE CONFIRMED — proposed period: 24 months], then destroy or anonymize them. If an engagement follows your request, the information needed for that engagement is kept according to the agreement that governs it.

## Security

We protect your information with measures suited to its sensitivity:

- encrypted connection across the entire website;
- access limited to the people who need it, with named accounts and least privilege;
- server-side spam verification and authenticated transmission of forms;
- no health information collected by the website.

No measure eliminates every risk. We review ours regularly.

## Your rights

At any time, you can:

- ask for access to the information we hold about you;
- ask for it to be corrected if it is inaccurate, incomplete or equivocal;
- withdraw your consent;
- ask that computerized information collected from you be communicated to you in a structured, commonly used technological format (portability);
- file a complaint with the [Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/).

To exercise these rights, write to our person in charge of the protection of personal information. We respond within the time limit set by law. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

## Confidentiality incidents

We keep a register of confidentiality incidents. If an incident presents a risk of serious injury, we notify the Commission d’accès à l’information and the persons concerned, and we take steps to reduce its consequences.

## Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. The date of the last update appears on this page. If there is a significant change, we will indicate it clearly on the website.
