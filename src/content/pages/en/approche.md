h1: "A four-step method, from diagnostic to autopilot"
lead: "We understand your operations before we touch your tools. Then we move step by step, testing and documenting everything we put in place."
steps:
  h2: "The four steps"
  items:
    - text: "We map your actual operations."
      details:
        - "Interviews with the people who do the work day to day."
        - "An inventory of your tools, your files and your data exchanges."
        - "Identification of re-entry, delays and points of friction."
        - "An estimate of the time each pain point costs."
    - text: "We prioritize what pays off fastest."
      details:
        - "Each project ranked by expected gain and required effort."
        - "Recommended tool choices, starting from the ones you already have."
        - "A step-by-step plan, approved with you before anything is put in place."
    - text: "We connect, automate and test."
      details:
        - "Integrations between your applications and automated flows."
        - "Named accounts and least-privilege access."
        - "Testing with your data and real cases before going live."
        - "A phased go-live, to limit the impact on your operations."
    - text: "We document, train and monitor."
      details:
        - "Documentation of every flow, handed over to your team."
        - "Training for the people who use the tools day to day."
        - "Flow monitoring and alerts when an error occurs."
        - "Access removed at the end of the engagement."
agnostic:
  h2: "Platform-agnostic, on principle"
  text: "We don’t resell software. We start from your processes, then choose and connect the right tools, including the ones you already have."
  points:
    - "We don’t resell licences."
    - "Your current tools are the starting point."
    - "Every tool recommendation is justified by your processes."
    - "You keep your data, your access and your documentation."

  h2: "What you receive"
  items:
    - title: "Documentation"
      text: "Every flow is described: what it does, where it runs and how to change it. This documentation is handed over to you at the end of the engagement."
      icon: "file-text"
    - title: "Training"
      text: "Your teams learn to use and adjust what was put in place, working from their own cases."
      icon: "users"
    - title: "Monitoring"
      text: "After go-live, we monitor the flows to catch errors and fix them."
      icon: "timer"
thread:
  title: "The common thread"
  text: "The common thread: automated flows and integrations between your applications. At every step, the goal stays the same: information entered once flows on its own to where it is needed."
