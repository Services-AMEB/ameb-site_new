## Our commitment

Services AMEB inc. aims for level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2. This is a voluntary commitment: an accessible website is a better-designed website for everyone.

## Measures in place

- **Contrast.** Body text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 with its background. No information is conveyed by colour alone.
- **Keyboard.** All features can be used with a keyboard. The focus indicator remains visible and is never hidden by the header. A skip link leads directly to the main content.
- **Reduced motion.** If your device asks to reduce motion, the website directly displays the final state of each animation.
- **Animations.** The home page animations loop continuously, with no sound or rapid flashing. They stop entirely when your device asks to reduce motion.
- **Captions.** The website contains no video or audio content. Captions therefore do not apply for now.
- **Text alternatives.** Meaningful images and diagrams have a text description. Decorative elements are ignored by screen readers.
- **Forms.** Every field has a visible label. Errors are described in text and linked to the field concerned.
- **Structure.** Each page has a single main heading, a heading hierarchy, identified navigation regions and a declared language.

## How we test

- **Automated tests.** Accessibility checking tools run on every change to the website. A change that introduces a serious error is not published.
- **Manual tests.** We go through the website with a keyboard and with screen readers. Date of the last manual test: [TO BE CONFIRMED].

## Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some elements may present difficulties:

- the spam verification on forms (Cloudflare Turnstile) is provided by a third party, and its accessibility depends on that provider. If it blocks you, write to us directly by email;
- the animated diagrams summarize processes. Their content is repeated in text, but some visual details may be simplified there.

## Report a barrier

If an element of the website prevents you from accessing information or a service, let us know by email: [EMAIL TO BE CONFIRMED].

To help us fix it quickly, please include, if possible:

- the page address;
- what you were trying to do;
- the browser and, if applicable, the assistive technology used.

We can also send you the information you are looking for in another format.
